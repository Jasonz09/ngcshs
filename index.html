NG | CSHS

North Gwinnett High School's National Computer Science Honor Society helps students build coding confidence, serve the community, compete with friends, and discover where technology can take them.

No experience needed Freshmen can join, learn, and grow at a comfortable pace.
Real opportunities Members practice coding, compete, and earn volunteer hours.
Recognition that matters Eligible members can work toward honor-society graduation cords.

What We Do

NG CSHS helps students turn curiosity into useful skills, friendships, and service.

🎓

Learn With Support

Explore programming, problem solving, and computer science ideas through approachable meetings, examples, and practice.

🤝

Find Your People

Meet students who enjoy technology, help each other improve, and make coding feel less intimidating.

💡

Build Your Future

Gain experience for classes, competitions, college applications, service goals, and future STEM pathways.

Why Join NG CSHS?

A smart first step into computer science

NG CSHS is for students who are curious about coding, technology, and creative problem solving. You do not have to be an expert to start; the club is built to help members learn, contribute, and feel confident trying new things.

For incoming freshmen Join early to meet supportive upperclassmen, learn what computer science classes are like, and build skills before courses get more advanced.
For parents and guardians This is a club with practical benefits: structured activities, leadership opportunities, service hours, and a positive academic community.
🏆

Competitive Activities

Practice problem solving and teamwork through coding challenges, club competitions, and tech-focused events.

💻

Coding Experience

Build confidence with programming concepts that can help in computer science classes and personal projects.

🤲

Volunteer Hours

Use technology and service opportunities to give back while earning meaningful volunteer experience.

🎓

Graduation Cord

Eligible members can work toward honor-society recognition that celebrates commitment and participation.

🧠

CS Knowledge

Learn vocabulary, tools, and problem-solving habits that make computer science feel more understandable.

🍪

Monthly Premium Snacks

Enjoy a friendly meeting environment with snacks, conversation, and time to connect with other members.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

⚖️

Equity

We believe in creating equal opportunities for all students to learn, grow, and succeed in computer science, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's voice is heard.

🤲

Service

We use our technical skills to give back to our community, creating solutions that help others and make a positive impact in the world.

🌟

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in everything we do, from coding projects to community service, always pushing ourselves to grow and improve.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

⚖️

Equity

We believe in creating equal opportunities for all students to learn, grow, and succeed in computer science, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's voice is heard.

🤲

Service

We use our technical skills to give back to our community, creating solutions that help others and make a positive impact in the world.

🌟

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in everything we do, from coding projects to community service, always pushing ourselves to grow and improve.
